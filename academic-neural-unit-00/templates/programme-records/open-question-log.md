## 1. What Belongs Here

`research-programme-chartering.md` §2 routes a question here rather than into a programme when it
**has no falsifiable failure condition yet**. That is the primary intake. A question logged here is
not rejected — it is parked pending refinement, and refinement into a chartered programme is the
expected outcome for many of these.

Also logged here:

- A question returned from ratification for scoping (`research-programme-charter.md` §8).
- A depth gap or adjacent question surfaced mid-programme that is out of that programme's scope but
  worth keeping.

**Not logged here:** production-tooling needs discovered mid-research — those are referrals, and go
to `referral-note.md`. A question that fails the stage-of-inquiry test is not parked either; it is
out of ANU-00's charter and should be recorded as such in §3 with its reasoning, so the same
question does not get re-proposed later.
