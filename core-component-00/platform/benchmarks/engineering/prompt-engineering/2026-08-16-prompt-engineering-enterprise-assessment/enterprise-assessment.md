## Compliance Verdict

**Conditional — P1 gaps open, no P0.**

Layer 1 does not currently stand up against external practice on the dimensions that matter most
for a knowledge layer. Its two strongest-looking assets are weaker than they appear: the eval
harness's stability classifier is arithmetic on prompt text rather than measurement of model
behaviour, and the CoT classifier's rationale misattributes a real paper's real numbers to a
comparison that paper never ran. Neither meets the ASGF P0 wording — there is no production
consumer, no load trigger, and no session-length trigger anywhere in this module — so the verdict
is Conditional rather than Below Standard. The single biggest lever is R1 plus R2: both defects
are failures of grounding rather than of ambition, and correcting the empirical basis of the two
implementations is cheaper than the sophistication of their design would suggest.

The pattern documentation itself is in better shape than the code, but it is dated in one specific
way — it treats prompt-level instruction as the mechanism for structured output, and prompt
versioning as a future direction, at a point when external practice treats the first as legacy and
the second as table stakes.

### Evidence Completeness Statement

Of twelve benchmark rows, **nine carry a `Verified` external source** (B1–B5, B7–B10), **one is
`Unassessed — no source`** (B6), and **two are `N/A`** by module type (B11, B12). Twenty external
excerpts and twelve internal excerpts were copied verbatim from sources opened this session; one
external claim was sought and not found and is logged as S12 rather than dropped.

One dimension I considered important could not be sourced: whether keying a CoT decision on a

`Verified` excerpt, and I have none.
